# Welcome!

Thank you for visiting my blog. I’ve started different versions of this project over the past few years and never could quite setting in on a topic that excited me enough to follow through. I’m still not sure what this blog is going to look like in the future, but I know I wanted to start it for real this time.  

There are two topics I love to talk about most: drug hunting and computing. As a profession computational medicinal chemist, this really worked out for me. I’m also a father and working parent, so there may be some of that sprinkled in as well. The blog will change as life ebbs and flows, but I hope you at least find something interesting.
