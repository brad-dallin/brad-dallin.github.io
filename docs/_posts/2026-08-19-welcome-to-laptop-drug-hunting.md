# Welcome to Laptop Drug Hunting

## Exploring the Digital Frontier of Drug Discovery

Thank you for visiting my blog dedicated to computational drug hunting. This blog represents my journey through the fascinating intersection of chemistry, biology, and computer science that's transforming how we discover and develop new medicines.

### Why This Blog Exists

The landscape of drug discovery has changed dramatically in recent decades. What once required massive laboratories, expensive equipment, and large teams can now, in many aspects, be explored from a semi-reliable laptop. As computing power increased and software became more accessible, the democratization of computational science has opened doors for independent researchers, students, and curious minds everywhere.

Here, I'll be sharing topics in computational medicinal chemistry that I find particularly interesting and, importantly, that can be approached with resources available to most individuals with a laptop and internet connection. No supercomputers or specialized hardware required!
